# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

This is a cross-platform terminal UI for Linux, macOS, and Windows. `web` is the neutral Impeccable platform classification because its schema has no terminal value; browser-specific live mode does not apply.

## Users

CTech Poker serves two equally important audiences: existing players who prefer operating from a terminal, and developers or other technical users who discover the game through the CLI. Both need a keyboard-first interface that makes the current table state and available actions immediately understandable.

## Product Purpose

The CLI brings the full CTech Poker play loop to the terminal rather than offering a reduced companion. It supports sandbox poker today and may support real-money play in the future. Success means players can authenticate, find and join a table, play a hand, inspect their cards, chat and react, follow achievements and summaries, understand outcomes, and recover from connection problems without leaving the terminal.

## Positioning

A complete, dependable poker experience built specifically for the terminal—not a novelty or a reduced version of the main product.

## Brand Personality

Focused, dependable, sociable, and understatedly playful. The product should inherit behavior and terminology from the main CTech Poker UI while drawing its interaction fluency from Claude Code and the Codex CLI.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a noisy casino interface. Avoid visual clutter, gratuitous spectacle, and decorative effects that compete with the table state or the player's next decision.

## Design Principles

- Keep the game state and the player's next valid actions immediately legible.
- Make frequent actions fast and predictable from the keyboard.
- Preserve behavioral parity with the main CTech Poker product while adapting interactions to the terminal.
- Communicate errors, retries, and reconnection state clearly without interrupting the player's mental model.
- Express poker personality through concise language and social moments, not casino clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support complete keyboard-only operation, `NO_COLOR`, and color-independent cues for status and meaning. Keep motion restrained, preserve readable hierarchy without relying on decoration, and ensure the interface remains usable in narrow and short terminal windows.
